# Cadence

A course marketplace where learners buy courses and instructors publish them.

## Language

### Purchases

**Refund**:
A learner handing a Purchase back to us and getting their money back, which our team reviews by hand.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation, Chargeback, Pending Chargeback, Refund Request

**Refund status**:
Where a Refund stands: **Requested** (the learner asked, nobody has decided), **Approved** (money goes back) or **Declined** (nothing goes back).
_Avoid_: Pending, Open

**Refund window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund can be asked for; the fourteenth day counts in full.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can get a Refund right now: inside the Refund window, no Refund already Requested, under two hours of video watched, under half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refund amount**:
What the learner paid for the Purchase being refunded — never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, cancellation amount
